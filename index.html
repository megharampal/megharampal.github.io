01THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Creative thinking.
Strategic communication.
Hands-on execution.

Hi, I’m Megha.

With 16+ years of experience across marketing, corporate communications and brand management, I bring together creative thinking, strategic communication and hands-on execution to build impactful brand experiences.

My cross-industry experience spans IT, education, retail, management consulting, engineering and sustainability. I began my career as a graphic designer in education and evolved into a multifaceted communications professional working across internal and external communications, Global MarCom, digital branding, content creation and stakeholder engagement.

My expertise spans brand messaging and positioning, corporate communications, internal engagement, social media strategy, email marketing, digital and print campaigns, proposals and presentations, infographics, video editing, conference branding and audiovisual (AV) management. From developing campaign concepts and communication strategies to managing end-to-end execution, I focus on brand consistency, audience relevance and business impact.

Beyond creation, I enjoy shaping the bigger picture: understanding the audience, defining the message and ensuring every communication touchpoint serves a clear purpose. Whether developing visual identities, creating executive presentations, managing stakeholders and vendors, or orchestrating corporate sales conferences from branding to AV execution, I combine creative problem-solving with meticulous planning and delivery.

A visual storyteller and communicator at heart, I believe the strongest brands are built through clear messaging, thoughtful design and meaningful engagement. My approach balances strategic perspective with a hands-on mindset, adaptability and a collaborative spirit.
