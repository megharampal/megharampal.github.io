Hi, I’m Megha.

Marketing, Corporate Communications & Visual Story Teller

With 16+ years of experience in marketing, corporate communications, and brand management across diverse industries, I combine creative storytelling, strategic thinking, and hands-on execution to build impactful brand experiences. My expertise spans brand positioning, internal and external communications, global marketing communications, digital branding, content creation, social media, integrated campaigns, and stakeholder engagement.

Beginning my career as a graphic designer, I have evolved into a versatile communications professional who translates business objectives into compelling narratives, engaging visuals, and cohesive brand experiences. From developing communication strategies and executive presentations to managing corporate conferences, vendors, and AV execution, I bring together creativity, meticulous planning, and collaboration to deliver meaningful business impact.

As a visual storyteller, I believe strong brands are built through clear messaging, thoughtful design, consistent communication, and meaningful audience engagement.
